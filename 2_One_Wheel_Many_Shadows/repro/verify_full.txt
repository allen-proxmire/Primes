==============================================================================
  Balance Ratio §2.1 — 3-divisibility of the Eisenstein form
==============================================================================
  Identity: h1^2+h1h2+h2^2 = (h1-h2)^2 + 3h1h2, so mod 3 it is (h1-h2)^2.
  Exhaustive check of the form mod 3:
    3 | form  <=>  h1 == h2 (mod 3)   [counterexamples: 0]
  Reachable (g1,g2) mod 6 for consecutive primes > 3:
    g1 == g2 only for: [(1, 1, 1), (5, 5, 5)] — always g1=g2=0
    => 3 | form  <=>  three consecutive primes share a residue mod 6

  P(single repeat, p' = p mod 6)          = 43.002%
  P(3 | form) = P(triple repeat)          = 17.408%
  if transitions were independent          = 18.492%
  if residues were even odds               = 12.500%
