# 4 — Philosophy & Ontology

**What the mathematics might mean, kept deliberately separate from what it proves.**

Nothing in this folder is a result. It is where the interpretive work lives, so that it does not leak into the papers that claim things.

**Start with [The Movie](The_Movie.md)** — the whole collection in plain language, no notation. It is the best entry point to the entire repository, not just this folder: every prime is a clock, clocks join the orchestra at their own square, and the combined pattern outruns the stretch of road where it applies. That last fact is why the primes look random despite a rule you could write on a napkin.

**Also here:**

- [Counting Into Existence](Counting_Into_Existence.md) — a finitist reading, and the debt it owes: why does pretending there are infinities give right answers about a finite world?
- [The Wolves and the Clock](The_Wolves_and_the_Clock.md) — the sieve as a hunt.
- [Template and Amplitude](FS_TB_QM_Amplitude_Memo.md) — a disciplined quantum-mechanical reading, with [its own open question](OPEN_QUESTION_FS_TB_QM_AMP_02_Bilinearity.md) about where the analogy breaks.

**The standing rule for this folder:** a reading is allowed to be suggestive. It is not allowed to be cited as evidence anywhere else in the collection.
