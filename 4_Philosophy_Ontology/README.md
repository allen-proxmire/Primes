# 4 — Philosophy & Ontology

**What the mathematics might mean, kept deliberately separate from what it proves.**

Most of this folder is interpretation, not result — it is where the readings live, so they do not leak into the papers that claim things. **Two files are exceptions and are labelled as such:** [The Movie](The_Movie.md) and [The Ninety Percent Rule](The_Ninety_Percent_Rule.md) are plain-language *restatements of measured work*, each carried by a reproduction script and by technical documents elsewhere in the collection.

**Start with [The Movie](The_Movie.md)** — the whole collection in plain language, no notation. It is the best entry point to the entire repository, not just this folder: every prime is a clock, clocks join the orchestra at their own square, and the combined pattern outruns the stretch of road where it applies. That last fact is why the primes look random despite a rule you could write on a napkin.

**Also here:**

- [**The Ninety Percent Rule**](The_Ninety_Percent_Rule.md) — **the prediction result, in full.** Carry four wheels (2, 3, 5, 7); walk forward testing only the open positions. How many before you catch the next prime nine times out of ten? **3 near $10^3$, 7 near $10^6$, 11 near $10^{10}$** — one extra slot per decade, measured at every decade. And the count is *predicted* by $q = 4.375/\ln N$ plus a geometric distribution, matching the measurement at all eight. Includes what it does **not** do: it predicts effort, never identity. Reproduce with [`repro/ninety_percent_table.py`](repro/ninety_percent_table.py).
- [Counting Into Existence](Counting_Into_Existence.md) — a finitist reading, and the debt it owes: why does pretending there are infinities give right answers about a finite world?
- [The Wolves and the Clock](The_Wolves_and_the_Clock.md) — the sieve as a hunt.
- [Template and Amplitude](FS_TB_QM_Amplitude_Memo.md) — a disciplined quantum-mechanical reading, with [its own open question](OPEN_QUESTION_FS_TB_QM_AMP_02_Bilinearity.md) about where the analogy breaks.

**The standing rule for this folder:** a reading is allowed to be suggestive. It is not allowed to be cited as evidence anywhere else in the collection. The two labelled exceptions above are held to the ordinary standard instead — every number in them is reproducible from a script in this repository.
